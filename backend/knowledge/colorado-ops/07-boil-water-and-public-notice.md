# Boil-water and public notice pointers (Colorado)

Source: CDPHE drinking water resources (curated extract)
Online: https://cdphe.colorado.gov/drinking-water
Retrieved: 2026-08-04

## When this matters

Public notice and boil-water advisories are **regulatory / emergency communications**. Water Saver does not issue them.

## Operator takeaways

1. Start at the CDPHE drinking water hub: https://cdphe.colorado.gov/drinking-water
2. Contact CDPHE Drinking Water for situation-specific direction: 303-692-3541 · Cdphe.drinkingwater@state.co.us
3. Follow your system’s emergency response / public notice procedures and CDPHE instructions — not chat summaries alone.

## AI assistant rule

If Context is thin, say you do not have enough information to draft a public notice. Point the operator to CDPHE and their certified operator / ORC.
