## Operator takeaways

1. Follow the **monitoring schedule** for your system — schedules are updated weekly:
   https://wqcdcompliance.com/schedules
2. Revised Total Coliform Rule (RTCR) and related sampling guidance:
   https://cdphe.colorado.gov/rtcr
3. Site-specific approved plans and certified operator judgment control — chat is educational support only.

## Calm next step

Open your current schedule online, confirm sample sites and due dates, and document who collects each sample. Do not invent sample frequencies from this extract alone.
