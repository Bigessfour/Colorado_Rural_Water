# Seasonal and intermittent public water systems (pointers)

Source: CDPHE RTCR / drinking water hub (curated extract)
Online: https://cdphe.colorado.gov/rtcr · https://cdphe.colorado.gov/drinking-water
Retrieved: 2026-08-04

## Operator takeaways

1. Seasonal systems often have extra start-up / shut-down sampling and notice requirements — see RTCR materials:
   https://cdphe.colorado.gov/rtcr
2. Confirm whether your system operates year-round or seasonally with CDPHE schedules:
   https://wqcdcompliance.com/schedules
3. Do not invent start-up residual or flush procedures from this chat extract.

## Calm next step

If you are opening or closing a seasonal system, call your certified operator and CDPHE contacts listed in `01-online-sources.md` before changing treatment.
