## What this hub covers

Public water system (PWS) operators should start here for Colorado drinking-water compliance tools:

- Compliance assurance — monitoring schedules, draft Consumer Confidence Reports, certified laboratory list
- Facility design approval — design review submittals and guidance
- Facility operator certification — CCWP Portal, training units, ORC responsibilities (see also https://cdphe.colorado.gov/wq-facility-operator)
- Grants and loans for public water systems
- Inspection services — sanitary survey; non-community groundwater
- Source water assessment and protection (SWAP)
- Training, coaching, and Pursuing Excellence awards
- Guidance and forms; online Portal for data submission
- Publications (Aqua Talk, fees, incident reports)
- Regulations, policies, and guidance for public water systems

## When is a system a “public water system”?

Generally, CDPHE regulates systems that operate 60 days or more per year and provide water for human consumption either:

- On average at least 25 people per day during the 60 busiest days, **or**
- Via 15 or more service connections

EPA: human consumption includes drinking, bathing, showering, cooking, dishwashing, or maintaining oral hygiene. Access includes faucets, restrooms, hose bibs, etc.

Private residences with individual private wells are usually **not** public water systems.
