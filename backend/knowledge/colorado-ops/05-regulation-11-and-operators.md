## Regulation 11 (drinking water)

- Colorado’s primary drinking-water rulebook for public water systems.
- Official text is maintained via the Secretary of State link from: https://cdphe.colorado.gov/water-quality-control-commission-regulations
- Residual / disinfection expectations used in Water Saver guidance are summarized in `03-disinfectant-residual.md` with links to https://cdphe.colorado.gov/dbps and https://cdphe.colorado.gov/rtcr — always verify against current Regulation 11.

## Operator in responsible charge (ORC)

From https://cdphe.colorado.gov/wq-facility-operator :

- No owner shall allow a water or wastewater facility to operate without direct supervision of an ORC certified at a classification equal to or higher than the facility (Regulation 100 §100.18). Civil penalty noted on that page: $300 per day per violation.
- ORC is designated by the owner and has supervisory responsibility for operation and for other facility operators.
- Drinking water facility contact update form is linked from the operator certification page.

## Clean water vs drinking water

- **Drinking water (PWS):** start at https://cdphe.colorado.gov/drinking-water
- **Clean water (surface water / discharge / permits):** https://cdphe.colorado.gov/clean-water — generally **not** the first stop for meter / residual / MOR questions in Water Saver.
