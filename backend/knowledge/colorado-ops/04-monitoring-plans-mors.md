## Monitoring plans (https://cdphe.colorado.gov/monitoringplans)

- Use the **monitoring plan wizard** (or paper forms) then submit through the CDPHE Portal.
- Portal manages distribution sample sites for DBPs, lead and copper, and total coliform.
- System / source type appear at the top of the monitoring schedule; general information below.
- Entry-point disinfectant residual, chemicals, nitrate/nitrite, radionuclides, and bromate are part of **inventory** (not separate plan sections).
- **Schedules:** all routine monitoring info, facilities, sample points (state IDs), and classifications → https://wqcdcompliance.com/schedules (check regularly; weekly updates).

### Change / emergency notes

- Review and update sample sites when the system changes (population, new sources, treatment changes). Contact CDPHE **before** making system changes. Rule-related sampling plan changes must be sent within **30 calendar days** of the effective date.
- Emergency facility start-up (summary from CDPHE page): contact within **24 hours**; entry-point coliform within **24 hours**; entry-point nitrate within **48 hours**.

### Reporting deadlines (general)

- For most rules, results must be submitted within the first **10 calendar days** following the month the result is received, **or** within the first 10 calendar days after the monitoring period ends — whichever is shorter.
- Labs may report for the system, but the **system remains responsible** for timely, complete reporting.
- Use certified labs for the specific method/analysis (EPA or CDPHE certified). See Laboratory Services Division lists from the drinking-water hub.

## Monthly operating reports (https://cdphe.colorado.gov/mors)

- Turbidity and disinfectant MOR forms, Excel and online templates, instructional videos, checklist / SOP materials.
- Individual filter effluent turbidity reporting is only required for exceedances (per CDPHE MOR page).
- Operators should use the current forms linked from the live MOR page rather than outdated copies.

## RTCR / residual logs (https://cdphe.colorado.gov/rtcr)

Notable forms/guidance listed online include:

- RTCR sites and Groundwater Rule monitoring plan pieces
- Seasonal start-up certificate of delivery
- Level 1 assessment for RTCR
- Groundwater entry point chlorine residual disinfectant monitoring log
- Sample result correction / deletion / invalidation (including residual updates)
- Quick guides: Minimum disinfection for groundwater; Disinfectant residual
